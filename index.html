Formula

RPM = (SFM × 3.82) / Diameter
IPM = RPM × IPR

The constant 3.82 comes from SFM × 12 / (π × D) ≈ SFM × 3.82 / D (12 converts feet→inches; π≈3.1416 → 12/π≈3.8197).
